I am a Research Scientist in the Optimization and Intelligent Robotics (OR) group at [Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories](https://www.merl.com), led by [Dr. Arvind Raghunathan](https://www.merl.com/people/raghunathan). I am working on Human-Robot Collaboration within the loco-manipulation domain, using humanoid robots. Concurrently, I am also working on Contextual Active SLAM, to enable robots to autonomously navigate environments while accounting for semantic cues and user-directives through vision language and large language models.

Overall, I have strong hands-on expertise to ensure my research can be applied to real robotic systems, and have experience across a wide variety of robotic platforms which include: Bipeds (MOBIUS, Agibot), Quadrupeds (A1 Unitree, Go2 Unitree, ALPHRED), Manipulator Arms (MELFA, AgileX), Turtlebot, and Drones (CrazyFly).

I hold a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), advised by Professor Dennis Hong from the [Robotics and Mechanisms Laboratories](https://www.romela.org). My research primarily focused on unifying model and learning-based techniques for planning, control, and estimation of robotic systems, see my [Thesis](https://www.proquest.com/openview/707560d1916c9a2b31fd48d527134927/1?pq-origsite=gscholar&cbl=18750&diss=y) for more information. Overall, the works during my Ph.D. spanned from heterogeneous Active SLAM for multi-agent systems, force control for climbing loco-manipulation, state estimation of legged robots, to auto-tuning planners and control parameters for robotic systems.
